# Pact official account · introduction post

## Post

Meet Pact.

AI agents are starting to do real work: calling APIs, using tools, and making decisions that affect people and money. That is exciting. It also raises a simple question: who decides what an agent is allowed to do?

Pact gives that decision back to the people responsible. Connect an agent, choose the tools and endpoints it can use, set grants and policies, and ask for approval when an action needs a human. Pact checks a request before it reaches the service, then shows what happened and why.

For supported actions on Monad, Pact can carry that record further—from authority and obligations through settlement to a receipt that others can verify. Developers can integrate through our SDKs and MCP server.

We want agents to be useful in the real world without making trust a leap of faith. This is the beginning of Pact. Explore it, try it, and tell us what you would want to put behind a clear boundary.
