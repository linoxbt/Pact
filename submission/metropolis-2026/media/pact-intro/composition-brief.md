# Hyperframes composition brief

Four independent six-second, seek-safe Hyperframes/GSAP chapters are generated by `build.mjs`. Each uses native `data-composition-id`, `data-start`, `data-duration`, and track metadata. Run Hyperframes `check` on all chapters before rendering. The product captures come from existing Pact assets; they must not be represented as a new authenticated transaction recording.

Render at 1280 × 720, 30 fps. Preserve Pact's warm paper, forest and mint palette. Keep text readable during camera moves. `assemble.py` joins the renders, mixes music and two interface sounds, extracts a settled poster, and bakes that poster into frame zero.
